📋 The Requirement — AXIOS & BSC Endoscopy

One question for a clinical risk leader: "What is the probability that an AXIOS / BSC endoscopy procedure results in a reported complication?" Boston Scientific can see complaints internally — but cannot compute procedures performed (the denominator), hospital-level outcomes, or a normalised rate. The three CMS datasets below fix that.

{# ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ DATASET 1 — Medicare Physician & Other Practitioner Data ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ #}
Dataset 1 — Medicare Physician & Other Practitioner Data · Public, Free, Available Now

Publicly available at data.cms.gov with no special access required. Contains the number of times each CPT procedure code was billed to Medicare per year, aggregated by provider and geography.

Directly relevant CPT codes for AXIOS / BSC endoscopy: CPT 43260 diagnostic ERCP · CPT 43264 ERCP w/ removal of calculi · CPT 43274–43278 stent placement and removal — all directly relevant to AXIOS stent procedures.

What we compute below: total AXIOS-relevant procedure volume by CPT, by geography, by institution type. This gives us the denominator for probability-of-occurrence estimates.

{# ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ DATASET 2 — CMS Hospital Compare / Complications and Deaths ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ #}
Dataset 2 — CMS Hospital Compare / Complications and Deaths · Public, Free

Available at data.cms.gov without special access. Contains hospital-level complication rates and 30-day readmission rates for specific procedure categories including GI procedures.

What we compute below: Which hospitals performing ERCP-family procedures have above-average complication rates? Cross-referenced with MAUDE complaint geography (further down), this creates a signal no single manufacturer can produce internally — it shows whether complaint clusters correlate with high-complication hospitals.

Honest caveat: CMS does not publish ERCP-specific complication rates. The five measures below are the closest proxies for the actual top AXIOS complications: PSI-15 (perforation), PSI-09 (bleeding), PSI-13 (sepsis → biliary infection), PSI-90 (composite safety), READM_30_HOSP_WIDE (all-cause readmission).

Dataset 3 — Medicare Part B Public Use File · Public, Annual Release

Annual aggregate data on procedure volumes and Medicare payments by HCPCS/CPT code. Available publicly. Shows procedure volume trends over time nationally.

What we compute below: Is the AXIOS-relevant procedure volume growing or shrinking? Combined with MAUDE complaint velocity, this normalises our complaint rate — instead of "complaints are rising," we say "complaint rate per procedure performed is rising," which is the more defensible and more alarming metric for a clinical Risk leader's risk file.
