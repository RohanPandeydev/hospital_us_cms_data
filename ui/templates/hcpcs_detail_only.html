⚖Is this a "Designated Health Service"?

The Stark Law stops doctors from referring Medicare patients to a service that they (or their family) have a financial interest in. CMS publishes a yearly list of services this rule applies to — called Designated Health Services (DHS). If a code shows up here, it's subject to extra anti-self-referral compliance rules.
