🔢 HCPCS / CPT Code Dictionary

Master lookup for every Medicare procedure, device, supply, and drug code. The link between "what was billed" and "what device was used."

HCPCS (Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System) has two levels. Level I = CPT codes (5-digit numeric, owned by the AMA — e.g. 27130 = total hip replacement). Level II = CMS codes (letter + 4 digits — e.g. C1741 = bone fixation anchor implant, L8699 = implanted prosthetic device). Together they cover everything Medicare pays for. We use them to bridge Medicare billing (CCN + HCPCS) to FDA device reports (product_code + brand).
