🔬 FDA MAUDE — Device Adverse Events

Reported device malfunctions, injuries, and deaths. Source: FDA openFDA API (api.fda.gov).

What is MAUDE? The FDA's Manufacturer and User Facility Device Experience database. Every time a medical device is reported to fail, harm, or kill a patient, it shows up here. Each event has the device product_code, brand, manufacturer, model, and often a UDI barcode. These events are the device-side signal we correlate with hospital outcomes on the Linkage page.
