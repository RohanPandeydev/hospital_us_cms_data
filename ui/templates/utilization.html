💰 Medicare Utilization & Device Billing

Procedure volumes per hospital, DMEPOS device billing, and Medicare payments.

What this adds: CMS's provider-data tells us rates (readmissions, complications, infections) — but rates are meaningless without volume. This section gives us the denominator: how many hip/knee replacements, stents, cardiac devices, etc. each hospital actually performs. That lets us turn "N adverse events" into "N per 100 procedures" — comparable across facilities.

Source: data.cms.gov/data-api/v1
