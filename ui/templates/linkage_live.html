🔗 Device ↔ Hospital Outcome Linkage

Live join: FDA device adverse events × CMS hospital outcomes. This is the point of the whole system.

How the join works:
1. FDA reports a device event → gets us product_code (e.g. LWS = defibrillator)
2. Our bridge maps that product_code → CMS measure (e.g. READM-30-HF-HRRP)
3. That CMS measure has per-hospital values → we know which hospitals are at risk
