🏢 Boston Scientific Portfolio — HCPCS + Open Payments

Two clean lenses on Boston Scientific in the CMS data: HCPCS-keyed Medicare procedure billing (what BSC's devices are billed for and how often) and Open Payments (what BSC paid US physicians). Nothing else — no MAUDE, no quality measures.

Two source lenses on this page:
  • HCPCS / CMS — every HCPCS code mapped to a BSC FDA product code (via bridge_hcpcs_to_product_code), joined to cms_provider_summary for procedure volume from the Medicare Physician PUF.
  • Open Payments — every dollar BSC paid a US physician, from openpaymentsdata.cms.gov (loads to cms_open_payments).

📖 What this page does
  • For every BSC FDA product code (from MAUDE), find the matching HCPCS via the bridge.
  • Pull procedure volume for those HCPCS from the Physician PUF.
  • Pull every BSC-→-physician dollar from Open Payments.
  • Cross-reference is left to the eye — the same NPI may appear in both tables.
