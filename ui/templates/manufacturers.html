📉 Manufacturer Payment Trends

Year-over-year change in manufacturer → physician payments. Per the doc: a sustained 30-60% drop in royalty/consulting payments often appears 12-18 months before a major recall.

How to read it: We aggregate Open Payments by manufacturer × year. Subsidiaries are rolled up via manufacturer_alias (162 entries — Medtronic Vascular → Medtronic, etc.). Where the manufacturer also files 510(k)s, we link the relevant FDA product_codes.

What the YoY column means: red = ≥30% decline (potential pre-recall signal per doc), yellow = 10-30% decline, green = stable or growing. Caveats: 2022 and 2023 are currently smoke samples (10K rows each); the 2024 data is the full release (497K rows). Slope numbers will be noisy until a full backfill of 2022/2023.
