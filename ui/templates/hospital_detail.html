↩️How often patients come back within 30 days

Within 30 days of discharge, what fraction of patients ended up back in a hospital? Lower is better. There are two flavors of these scores: Raw % rates (e.g. READM_30_HF = 19.6 means 19.6% of heart-failure patients were readmitted) and Excess Readmission Ratios (e.g. READM-30-HF-HRRP = 1.05 means 5% more patients came back than CMS predicted — Medicare penalizes anything above 1.0).
