CMS × FDA Device Risk Intelligence

Every US hospital's Medicare outcomes, every reported device adverse event, and the connections between them.

"We're pulling the complete public record that Medicare keeps on every US hospital — 5,426 hospitals, 60,000 quality scores across 144 outcome measures — because it's the only dataset in existence that tells us how every hospital in America actually performs, and it's already labeled in the form an AI model can train on."
What you can do here: Find the worst-performing hospitals for any outcome → drill into their profile → see which device categories are tied to their risks. Designed for the question: "Where are devices failing, and which hospitals are most exposed?"
