🩺 Device Recall Predictor

Per-product-code risk dashboard, grouped by FDA risk class. Each row is one FDA product_code linked to its Medicare procedure volume (CMS Part B), recent MAUDE adverse-event activity, and FDA recall history.

How to read each table:
  • MAUDE 12mo / Prev 12mo / YoY % — adverse-event volume in the most recent 12 months, the prior 12 months, and the percent change. red ≥ +50% is a rising failure trend (the doc's complication-velocity signal).
  • Recalls 24mo — FDA recalls initiated in the last 24 months. A non-zero number plus a rising MAUDE % is the strongest combined signal in the doc (22% recall-probability uplift).
  • Rate / 1k procs — MAUDE events 12mo ÷ Part B annual procedures × 1000. Apples-to-apples 12-month-over-12-month — but Part B Physician misses inpatient and facility-billed procedures, so the denominator is partial for implantables.
