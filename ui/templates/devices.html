Per-product-code recall risk: Medicare procedure volume × FDA MAUDE adverse-event count × prior recalls. The doc's complaint_rate_per_1k_procedures feature, computed end-to-end.

How this is built: Part B Physician procedure volume (CMS, by HCPCS) ÷ MAUDE adverse-event count (FDA, by product_code), bridged via bridge_hcpcs_to_product_code. Higher rate = more reported failures per 1,000 billed procedures. Caveats: MAUDE is known to underreport (doc cites 30-50% capture); high-volume devices naturally show more raw events even when safe.
