Math: P(hospital | event) = hospital's national share of {{ cfg.label }} procedure volume. estimated_events = P × {{ hero[0] }} · estimated_deaths = P × {{ hero[1] }}.

Honest caveat: FDA redacts reporter facility name from public MAUDE per 21 CFR 803.9(b). We cannot say "this event happened at this hospital." We can say "this hospital probably had ~N {{ cfg.label }} events, given its share of procedure volume."

⚖️ What this dashboard can / cannot prove

✅ CAN prove: {{ cfg.label }} had {{ hero[0] }} adverse events ({{ hero[1] }} deaths) across MAUDE, with failure modes recorded.
✅ CAN show: Which hospitals do high {{ cfg.label }}-procedure volume AND carry safety penalties — defensible sales / risk targeting signal.
❌ CANNOT prove: "Event X happened at Hospital Y." FDA strips reporter facility name from public MAUDE. We provide probability estimates, not deterministic attribution. Verifying any specific event-to-hospital match requires Medicare LDS / ResDAC claims (DUA-restricted) or hospital purchase records.
