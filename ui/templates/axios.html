{# ── DATASET 2 — Hospital Compare ──────────────────────────────── #}
2️⃣ Hospital outcomes — proxy measures for ERCP risk · source: CMS Hospital Compare (PSI-90 family + HRRP)

Honest framing: CMS does not publish ERCP-specific complication rates at the hospital level. These five Patient Safety Indicators (PSIs) and the all-cause readmission rate are the closest proxies for the actual top AXIOS complications: perforation (PSI-15), bleeding (PSI-09), biliary infection (PSI-13), composite safety (PSI-90), and 30-day readmission (READM_30_HOSP_WIDE).

{# ── 🎯 The all-three-in-one row (manufacturer + device + CCN) ── #}
🎯 manufacturer + device + CCN — all in one row · source: vw_device_event_with_ccn (= fda_maude_devices ⨝ fda_maude_events ⨝ bridge_maude_to_ccn ⨝ cms_hospitals)

No single CMS dataset has manufacturer + device + CCN universally. This view fixes that by inferring the CCN on every MAUDE event from its facility_name + state, then joining the device row. The result is the requirement's "all three in one row" — for the entire United States, not just teaching hospitals (which is the limit of Open Payments).
